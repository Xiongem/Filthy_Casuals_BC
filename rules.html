Rules

1.

Have fun, don't be an ass.

2.

Don't be a sore loser.

3.

When reading e-books/cases where font size can be edited, page count should be taken from the physical edition if it is available. (If a book is dnf, take the reading percentage and multiply it by the physical page count. When rounding, use math rules.)

4.

Buddy reads only count if both readers are in the challenge.

5.

Pages read from a book you did not finish can still be counted.

6.

Freind Recs: both reader and recommender receive bonus points.

7.

Re-reading counts. Crack open all your comfort reads.

Activities

Buddy Read:
Grab a buddy and choose a book. The pages you read together can be marked as "buddy read" in the update page and count for extra points!
Friend Recommendation:
Check out Friend Recs to find your next read. Any pages read from a friend rec earn bonus points.
Read-a-thon:
One week a month will be designated as a read-a-thon week, mark everything you read during that time as "read-a-thon" in the update page and earn bonus points.
Seasonal:
Each quarter of the year
